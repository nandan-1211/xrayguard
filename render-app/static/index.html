PROJECT CONTRIBUTION

Reports that can be traced to model evidence

XRayGuard is an auditable workflow: the image model supplies evidence, templates turn it into sentences, and rules check the wording. The individual methods are established; the contribution is integrating them so every sentence can be traced and checked.

The analysis pipeline

  1. Image classificationDenseNet-121 (ImageNet-pretrained) scores 14 NIH findings, fine-tuned on a patient-separated NIH subset.
  2. Validation-derived thresholdsPer-finding temperature scaling and two thresholds decide whether a finding is stated, hedged or omitted. Precision is targeted on validation, not guaranteed on test.
  3. Prediction variability30 MC-dropout samples on the classifier head; unstable predictions are downgraded from "present" to "possible".
  4. Visual evidenceClass activation maps give approximate side and zone. No segmentation and no size or severity measurement.
  5. Structured reportingDeterministic templates or a constrained writer that sees only the evidence table; no confident negatives for sub-threshold findings.
  6. Consistency verificationA rule checker flags unsupported or overconfident statements, contradictions, laterality mismatches, sizes, prior-study references and devices, then corrects the draft.

Our model's results

DenseNet-121 trained on a 10,000-image NIH ChestX-ray14 subset and tested on 1,000 held-out images from 1,000 patients. Mean test AUROC 0.819 (95% CI 0.798–0.836). Findings are sorted by AUROC; accuracy, MSE, precision, recall and F1 use reporting thresholds chosen on the validation set.

Scope and responsible interpretation

  • Expects frontal PNG/JPEG chest X-rays.
  • NIH labels are mined from reports and contain noise.
  • Rare findings have few test examples.
  • Localisation, external-hospital and LLM-comparison experiments are incomplete.
  • On this demo page, image scores come from an AI vision model following the XRayGuard pipeline, not from the trained DenseNet.
  • Outputs are research drafts that need validation before any clinical use.
